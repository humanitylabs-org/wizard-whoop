Open Health

Your recovery, sleep, strain and workouts show in the Health app. This app only keeps the WHOOP sign-in and the synced copy.

Connect your WHOOP in five short steps. Everything happens here and on WHOOP's own site.

  1. You need an active WHOOP membership on any tier (One, Peak or Life). WHOOP's developer access, which this app uses, is free.

    No membership yet? See WHOOP memberships.

    1. Open the dashboard and sign in with your usual WHOOP email and password.
    2. If it asks you to create a Team, do it. Any team name works, for example your own name.

    Replace WHOOP keys

    Paste the Client ID and Client Secret from your app on the WHOOP Developer Dashboard. You sign in to WHOOP again afterwards; the synced history stays.

    Disconnect WHOOP?

    The app signs out of WHOOP and deletes its synced copy. Health stops showing WHOOP data. Your data on WHOOP is not touched.
